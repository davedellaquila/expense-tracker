Data cleanup

Older PDF imports sometimes glued statement text onto merchant names — section headers (like "JANE DOE #1234: Transactions"), debit-card prefixes (like "Purchase authorized on 08/15 …") or fine print (like "Important Information You Should Know …"). Scan finds those rows so you can review and fix them — with undo.
